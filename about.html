Our Origins & Ethos

A Heritage of Diplomatic Precision.

Maison Royal was founded in 2012 by former diplomatic logistics officers and royal household protocol coordinators. Having witnessed firsthand the unpredictable nature of generic ridesharing and inconsistent town car providers, we set out to build an elite transportation institute.

Every journey with Maison Royal is orchestrated with military rigor and five-star hospitality. We do not simply drive our passengers; we safeguard their schedule, their privacy, and their peace of mind between meetings and private flights.

  • Zero tolerance for schedule variance or tardiness
  • Legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreements for all personnel
  • In-house Master Driver Academy with 120-hour certification
  • Direct coordination with airport authorities and private FBO hangars
